The Watch Reborn

AUREL · Precision, Reimagined

A luxury dive watch powers up in the dark, takes itself apart into an exploded view (bezel, sapphire crystal, hands, dial, case, movement, rotor, caseback, straps) and rebuilds before the brand reveal. Animated in SVGator as a reusable template: the brand name and tagline are editable text layers.

Watch modelled and rendered in Blender (Cycles) from scratch. Fonts: Cormorant Garamond and Montserrat (SIL OFL). This video is a 1920×1080 preview rendered from the same keyframes as the SVGator project.
